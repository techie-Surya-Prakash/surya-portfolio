HOW TO ADD THE GAME PROJECT TO YOUR SPIDER-VERSE PORTFOLIO

1. Copy the "projects" folder into your existing surya-portfolio folder
   (next to index.html, style.css, script.js and assets/).
2. Open card-snippet.html, copy the card into the Projects section of your
   index.html  (or just change your GAME_MODE card link to href="projects/game.html").
3. Edit projects/game.html: replace the placeholder text with your real game details.
4. Commit and push to GitHub. Page URL:
   https://techie-surya-prakash.github.io/surya-portfolio/projects/game.html

This folder does NOT include your own index.html / style.css, so nothing of yours is overwritten.
